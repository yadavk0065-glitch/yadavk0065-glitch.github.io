About ReSell

Give Your Old Things a New Life.

Introduction to ReSell

ReSell is a clean, user-friendly second-hand buying and selling website designed especially for college students and local buyers. Whether you want to buy an affordable smartphone, an engineering textbook, a bicycle, or sell a laptop you no longer need, ReSell makes the process simple, fast, and transparent.

Project Objective

  • Provide a simple online platform to list and browse pre-owned products.
  • Help students save money by buying well-maintained second-hand items.
  • Connect buyers and sellers directly through WhatsApp without middlemen.
  • Demonstrate real-world front-end web development using only HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.

Why Second-Hand Shopping Is Useful

  • Saves Money: Buy branded phones, laptops, and books at a fraction of the retail price.
  • Reduces E-Waste: Extending the life of electronics keeps harmful waste out of landfills.
  • Earns Extra Cash: Sellers can recover money from items lying unused in their rooms.
  • Smart for College Life: Ideal for semester books, study tables, and campus bicycles.

How the Website Works

1

Browse & Search

Users explore featured items on the Home page or search and filter products by category on the Buy page.

2

Post a Product

Sellers submit product details and an optional image on the Sell page. JavaScript validates the form and saves the item in the browser's localStorage.

3

View & Contact

Buyers view complete product details in a popup modal and click "Contact Seller" to open a direct WhatsApp chat.

Technologies Used

HTML5

Structure & Content

Used semantic tags like <header>, <section>, <form>, and <footer> to build four linked pages.

CSS3

Design & Responsiveness

Used Flexbox, CSS Grid, CSS variables, media queries, and smooth keyframe animations for a modern Cashify-inspired look.

JavaScript

Interactivity & Storage

Used Vanilla JavaScript for live search, category & condition filters, price sorting, form validation, deleting listings, WhatsApp links, and localStorage persistence.

Student College Project Information

This website has been developed as a First-Year, First-Semester B.Tech Computer Science & Engineering (CSE) mini-project to practice core front-end web technologies without using any external framework, backend server, or external database.
